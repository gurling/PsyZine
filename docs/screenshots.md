# PsyZine screenshots

`UiContrastTest` renders the real journal detail components and adaptive navigation with synthetic entries in all 13 palettes. It writes PNGs to the target app’s external-files `contrast-screenshots/` directory and checks the rendered Frappé card/navigation pixels against the reference colors. `tools/run-device-checks.sh` collects these alongside the preview captures; no private journal or photos are needed.

Keep contrast changes in `ThemePalettes`: page background, solid card (`surfaceContainerLow`/`surfaceContainerHighest`) and raised navigation/popover (`surfaceContainer`/`surfaceContainerHigh`) are separate roles. Frappé uses `#303446`, `#292C3C` and `#414559`, respectively. `ThemeContrastTest` checks readable text and domain accents across every neutral surface and guards against cards blending into the page.

`PsyZineUiRegressionTest.captureEveryPaletteForVisualReview` renders synthetic preview cards in all 13 palettes and saves PNGs to the target app’s external-files `parity-screenshots` directory. Android CI pulls these into its per-API artifacts for visual review. All 13 PNGs were generated on the Pixel and the six CI suites passed. `bash tools/run-device-checks.sh` retains target external files and pulls them before the emulator action shuts down, preserving the test exit status even if capture collection fails. Synthetic palette output does not establish complete screen or clipping acceptance. They are test fixtures, not screenshots of a user’s private journal.

After device acceptance, capture the actual journal/detail with synthetic photo fixtures, photo editor acquisition/error/discard states, full-screen viewer, Appearance light/dark variants, notification collapsed/expanded/private-lock-screen states, widget small/large/empty/private states, and tablet navigation. Review large text, touch targets, contrast, and clipping. Add approved current phone captures to `metadata/en-US/images/phoneScreenshots/`. Keep real private journals out of captures.

Upstream screenshots were removed from the fork listing because they do not show PsyZine. No generated mockup is presented as a device capture and no visual pass is claimed before the review.
